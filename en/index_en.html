Republic of Rofia

— A peaceful micronation born from quietness and culture —

Basic Information

Name: Republic of Rofia

Founded: April 30, 2025

Form: A new type of micronation mainly existing on the internet

Territory: A flowerbed in the garden and digital web space

Population: Unknown (citizens are online participants)

Capital: Republic of Rofia

Languages: Japanese, English

National Flower: Strawberry 🍓

Flag Colors:

  • White — Snow, nobility, honesty, peace, purity (RGB: 255, 255, 255)
  • Red — Love, courage, energy, power, people (RGB: 213, 43, 30)
  • Blue — Honor, inner beauty, belief, loyalty, happiness (RGB: 0, 63, 171)

National Philosophy

"A peaceful nation where the country and people live together — promoting the importance of ‘nation’ and inspiring interest in it like never before."

  • Coexistence across freedom, nature, and the digital world
  • Neutrality and friendship with all

Culture & Features

  • National drink: Hot milk ☕
  • Promoting scientific advancement while dreaming of a green future
  • Respect and coexistence with diverse cultures worldwide
  • The strawberry represents honesty, cheerfulness, and charm of citizens
  • Territory primarily exists online rather than physically
  • Rofia is a “living nation” — the country itself can talk, laugh, and play

Official Character “Rofia”

A childish boy-like character. Kind, cheerful, and curious about everything.

Sample Quotes:

  • “Hello, everyone! I'm a new micronation in the making! Let’s be friends!”
  • “So many things to prepare!”
